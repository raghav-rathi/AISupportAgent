# When a person takes over

The assistant answers from the Fernhill help center and can act on your account after verifying your identity. Some cases need a person.

## Cases that go to a person

- A refund above what support can approve in chat
- Anything on a suspended account
- A report of unauthorized access or another security problem
- Legal and privacy requests, such as deleting personal data
- A billing dispute or a charge you do not recognize
- A question the help center does not answer

## What happens next

The assistant opens a ticket and gives you its ID, which starts with `ESC-`. The ticket holds your account, your plan, the reason and a summary of the conversation, so you do not have to explain again. A specialist replies by email to the contact address on the account.

## Ticket priority

| Priority | Used for |
|---|---|
| Urgent | Security incidents and a service that is down |
| High | Billing errors and anything blocking a whole team |
| Normal | Most requests |
| Low | Questions and feedback |

## Phone support

Fernhill does not offer phone support for Free and Pro workspaces. Enterprise customers can call their account manager on the number in the order form.
