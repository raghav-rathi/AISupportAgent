# Plans and pricing

Fernhill Workspace has three plans: Free, Pro and Enterprise. Every workspace is on exactly one plan.

## Plan comparison

| | Free | Pro | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | $0 | $12 per seat per month, or $120 per seat per year | Annual contract, quoted per seat |
| Seats | Up to 5 | Up to 100 | Set by contract |
| Active projects | 3 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Storage | 2 GB per workspace | 50 GB per seat | 1 TB pooled, more by add-on |
| Version history | 7 days | 90 days | Unlimited |
| Integrations | Slack | Slack, GitHub, Google Drive, Zapier, webhooks | Everything in Pro, plus custom integrations |
| Sign-in | Email and password | Email and password, Google sign-in | SAML single sign-on, SCIM provisioning |
| API rate limit | 60 requests per minute | 600 requests per minute | 6,000 requests per minute |
| Support | Help center and community forum | Email, first reply within 1 business day | 24/7, with a named account manager |

## Free plan

The Free plan costs nothing and never asks for a payment method. It suits small teams trying Fernhill: up to 5 seats, 3 active projects and 2 GB of storage for the whole workspace. Archived projects do not count toward the limit of 3.

## Pro plan

Pro is billed per seat. Monthly billing costs $12 per seat per month. Annual billing costs $120 per seat per year, which works out to two months free. A Pro workspace can have up to 100 seats. Teams that need more move to Enterprise.

## Enterprise plan

Enterprise is sold on an annual contract with a price per seat agreed in the order form. It adds SAML single sign-on, SCIM provisioning, an uptime commitment, 24/7 support and a named account manager. To get a quote, ask for the sales team and the assistant will open a request for you.

## Changing plans

1. Open **Settings > Billing > Plan**.
2. Pick the new plan and confirm.

An upgrade takes effect at once, and the first charge is prorated for the rest of the billing period. A downgrade takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for. Only workspace owners and admins can change the plan. Support cannot change a plan in chat.

## Switching between monthly and annual billing

A Pro workspace can switch billing cycle at any time in **Settings > Billing > Plan**. The switch takes effect at the next renewal date.
