# Account changes and cancellation

## What support can change in chat

After verifying your identity, the assistant can change these details on the account:

- Billing email
- Contact name
- Contact phone number
- Whether you get marketing emails

## What you change yourself

Owners and admins change these in the app:

| Change | Where |
|---|---|
| Plan and billing cycle | Settings > Billing > Plan |
| Payment method | Settings > Billing > Payment method |
| Seats and members | Settings > Members |
| Workspace name | Settings > Workspace > General |

## What needs a specialist

The assistant opens a ticket for these, because a person has to check them:

- Transferring ownership when the owner cannot do it
- Changing the legal company name on a contract or on past invoices
- Deleting a workspace or personal data for good
- Any change on a suspended account

## Cancelling a paid plan
<!-- tiers: pro, enterprise -->

### Pro
<!-- tiers: pro -->

1. Open **Settings > Billing > Plan**.
2. Select **Cancel plan** and confirm.

The plan stays active until the end of the period you have paid for. Then the workspace moves to the Free plan. You can undo the cancellation any time before that date by selecting **Resume plan**.

### Enterprise
<!-- tiers: enterprise -->

An Enterprise contract runs for its full term. To cancel a renewal, tell your account manager in writing at least 60 days before the renewal date.

## Suspended accounts

An account is suspended when a payment is disputed with the bank or when our terms are broken. A suspended workspace is read-only. Support cannot lift a suspension, issue refunds or change account details in chat. The assistant opens a ticket for the billing team.
