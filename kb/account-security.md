# Account security

## Resetting your password

1. Go to the sign-in page and select **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email address on your account.
3. Open the email from Fernhill and follow the link. The link works for 60 minutes.
4. Choose a new password of at least 12 characters.

If no email arrives within 10 minutes, check the spam folder and confirm that you used the address on the account. Support cannot see or set passwords.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication adds a 6-digit code from an authenticator app at sign-in.

1. Open **Settings > Security > Two-factor authentication**.
2. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app.
3. Enter the 6-digit code to confirm.
4. Save the 10 recovery codes somewhere safe. Each code works once.

## Lost your two-factor device

Sign in with one of your recovery codes, then set up two-factor authentication again on the new device.

If you have no recovery codes left, support has to confirm who you are before two-factor authentication is turned off. The assistant opens a ticket for this. For safety, the change takes effect 24 hours after approval.

## Active sessions

**Settings > Security > Sessions** lists every device that is signed in. Select **Sign out** next to a device to end its session, or **Sign out everywhere** to end all sessions except the current one.

## Suspicious activity

If you see a sign-in you do not recognize, change your password, sign out everywhere, and tell us. The assistant passes reports of unauthorized access to the security team as urgent.
