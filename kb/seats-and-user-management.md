# Seats and user management

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything, including billing, deleting the workspace and transferring ownership |
| Admin | Manage members, integrations, security settings and billing |
| Member | Create and edit projects, cards and documents |
| Guest | View and comment on the projects shared with them |

A workspace has exactly one owner.

## Seat limits

### Free
<!-- tiers: free -->

A Free workspace has up to 5 seats. Guests are not available on the Free plan.

### Pro
<!-- tiers: pro -->

A Pro workspace has up to 100 seats. Guests are free and do not use a seat. Each guest can be added to up to 3 projects.

### Enterprise
<!-- tiers: enterprise -->

The number of seats on Enterprise is set in the order form. Guests are free and unlimited. To add seats beyond the order form, contact your account manager.

## Adding seats on Pro
<!-- tiers: pro -->

1. Open **Settings > Members** and select **Invite**.
2. Enter the email addresses.
3. Confirm the charge shown.

Each new seat is charged at once, prorated for the days left in the billing period.

## Removing seats on Pro
<!-- tiers: pro -->

Deactivate a member in **Settings > Members**. The seat is released at the next renewal date. There is no refund or credit for a seat removed in the middle of a billing period.

## Transferring ownership

The current owner can hand over ownership in **Settings > Members**: open the menu next to an admin and select **Make owner**.

If the owner has left the company or cannot sign in, support has to confirm the request with the company. The assistant opens a ticket for this. Support cannot transfer ownership in chat.
