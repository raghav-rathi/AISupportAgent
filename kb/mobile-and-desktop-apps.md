# Mobile and desktop apps

## Where Fernhill runs

| App | Requires |
|---|---|
| Web | A supported browser |
| iPhone and iPad | iOS 16 or later |
| Android | Android 11 or later |
| Mac | macOS 13 or later |
| Windows | Windows 10 or later |

All apps are free on every plan and sign in with the same account.

## Working offline

The desktop and mobile apps keep the projects you opened in the last 14 days on the device. You can read and edit them offline. Changes are sent when the connection returns.

## Updating the apps

Mobile apps update through the App Store and Google Play. The desktop app checks for updates at launch and installs them when you quit. An app more than 6 months out of date can be asked to update before it connects.

## Signing in on a new device

1. Install the app and select **Sign in**.
2. Enter your email address, or choose your single sign-on provider.
3. Enter your two-factor code if your account uses one.
