# Data export and retention

## Exporting your data

Owners and admins can export a whole workspace.

1. Open **Settings > Workspace > Export**.
2. Select **Start export**.
3. Wait for the email with the download link. Large workspaces can take up to 2 hours.

The export is a ZIP file. Boards are CSV files, documents are Markdown files, and attachments keep their original format. The download link works for 7 days.

## Data after cancellation

### Free
<!-- tiers: free -->

When a Free workspace is deleted, its data is kept for 30 days and then removed for good.

### Pro
<!-- tiers: pro -->

When a Pro plan ends, the workspace moves to the Free plan and its data is kept for 90 days. Content over the Free limits is read-only during that time. After 90 days the content over the Free limits is removed.

### Enterprise
<!-- tiers: enterprise -->

When an Enterprise contract ends, data is kept for 180 days unless the order form sets a different period. Your account manager can arrange a final export.

## Backups

Fernhill backs up every workspace once a day and keeps each backup for 35 days. Backups protect against failures on our side. Support cannot restore a single card or document from a backup. Use version history for that.

## Deleting your data

A request to delete a workspace or personal data for good goes to the privacy team. The assistant opens a ticket. Deletion is completed within 30 days of the request and cannot be undone.
