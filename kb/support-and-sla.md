# Support and service levels

## Service status

Live status and incident history are at status.fernhill.example. You can subscribe there to email updates for incidents.

## Free plan support
<!-- tiers: free -->

Free workspaces use the help center, the community forum and this assistant. There is no email support and no guaranteed response time on the Free plan.

## Pro plan support
<!-- tiers: pro -->

Pro workspaces get email support at support@fernhill.example. Support hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 US Eastern time. The first reply arrives within 1 business day.

Pro does not include an uptime commitment or service credits.

## Enterprise plan support
<!-- tiers: enterprise -->

Enterprise workspaces get support 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and a named account manager.

| Priority | Meaning | First response |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | The service is down or unusable for most users | Within 1 hour |
| P2 | A major feature is broken and there is no workaround | Within 4 hours |
| P3 | A minor problem or a question | Within 1 business day |

### Uptime commitment

Enterprise includes a monthly uptime commitment of 99.9%. Planned maintenance announced at least 5 days ahead does not count as downtime.

| Monthly uptime | Service credit |
|---|---|
| Below 99.9% | 10% of the monthly fee |
| Below 99.0% | 25% of the monthly fee |
| Below 95.0% | 50% of the monthly fee |

To claim a credit, write to your account manager within 30 days of the incident. Credits are applied to the next invoice. They are not paid out as cash.
