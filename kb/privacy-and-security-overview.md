# Privacy and security overview

## How data is protected

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or newer and at rest with AES-256. Customer data is stored in data centers in the United States.

## Who can see your content

Only members of your workspace can see its content. Fernhill staff open customer content only to resolve a support ticket, and only with permission from an owner or admin. Each access is logged.

## Compliance documents
<!-- tiers: enterprise -->

Fernhill holds a SOC 2 Type II report. Enterprise customers can get the report and a signed data processing agreement from their account manager. The report is shared under a non-disclosure agreement.

## Audit log

### Pro
<!-- tiers: pro -->

Pro workspaces keep an audit log of sign-ins, member changes and security settings for 30 days. Owners and admins see it in **Settings > Security > Audit log**.

### Enterprise
<!-- tiers: enterprise -->

Enterprise workspaces keep the audit log for 1 year and can export it as CSV or stream it to a SIEM through the API.

## Reporting a security problem

To report a vulnerability, write to security@fernhill.example. If you believe someone has accessed your workspace without permission, tell the assistant. It passes the report to the security team as urgent.
