# Refund policy

Refund terms depend on your plan. Verify your account in chat and the assistant will apply the terms for your plan.

## How refunds are paid

A refund goes back to the payment method that paid the invoice. It reaches a card in 5 to 10 business days. Each invoice can be refunded once, and always in full. Support does not issue partial refunds in chat.

A refund does not cancel a subscription. To stop future charges, cancel the plan in **Settings > Billing > Plan**.

## Free plan
<!-- tiers: free -->

Free workspaces are never charged, so there is nothing to refund. If a charge from Fernhill appears on a card and your workspace is on the Free plan, tell us. The assistant will pass the case to the billing team.

## Pro plan refunds
<!-- tiers: pro -->

| Billing cycle | Refund window |
|---|---|
| Monthly | 14 days from the invoice date |
| Annual | 30 days from the invoice date |

Inside the window you get the full amount of the invoice back, whatever the reason. The window is counted from the invoice date and includes the last day. After the window an invoice cannot be refunded. The plan stays active until the end of the paid period.

### What support can approve on Pro

Support can refund a Pro invoice of up to $500 on the spot. A larger invoice goes to the billing team, who reply within 2 business days. The refund window still applies.

## Enterprise plan refunds
<!-- tiers: enterprise -->

Enterprise invoices can be refunded within 30 days of the invoice date. This covers add-on purchases such as extra storage, onboarding packages and training. The window includes the last day.

The annual license follows the terms of your order form. If the order form and this page disagree, the order form wins.

### What support can approve on Enterprise

Support can refund an Enterprise invoice of up to $2,000 on the spot. A larger invoice goes to your account manager and the billing team. The refund window still applies.

## Duplicate charges
<!-- tiers: pro, enterprise -->

A duplicate charge is two paid invoices for the same billing period and the same amount. One of the two can be refunded in full up to 90 days after the invoice date, even when the normal refund window has passed. The support limit for your plan still applies.

## When a refund cannot be issued in chat
<!-- tiers: pro, enterprise -->

- **Suspended account.** Refunds on a suspended account need a review by the billing team. The assistant opens a ticket.
- **Unpaid balance.** An account that is past due gets refunds once the open invoice is paid.
- **Unpaid invoice.** An invoice that was never paid has nothing to refund.
- **Already refunded.** An invoice is refunded at most once.
