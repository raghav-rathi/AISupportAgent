# Billing and invoices

## Payment methods

### Pro
<!-- tiers: pro -->

Pro workspaces pay by card. We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express. Pro plans cannot be paid by bank transfer or check.

### Enterprise
<!-- tiers: enterprise -->

Enterprise customers pay by card or by bank transfer against an invoice. Invoices are due 30 days after the invoice date.

## Finding your invoices

Invoices are emailed to the billing email on the invoice date. To download one later:

1. Open **Settings > Billing > Invoices**.
2. Find the invoice by date or number. Invoice numbers start with `INV-`.
3. Select **Download PDF**.

Only owners and admins can see the Billing page.

## When a payment fails

When a card is declined we retry the charge 3 times over 7 days: on day 1, day 3 and day 7 after the first failure. The account is marked past due as soon as the first attempt fails.

| Days past due | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0 to 14 | Full access. Owners and admins see a banner |
| 15 to 30 | The workspace becomes read-only |
| After 30 | The workspace moves to the Free plan |

To fix a failed payment:

1. Open **Settings > Billing > Payment method**.
2. Select **Update card** and enter the new details.
3. Select **Retry payment** next to the open invoice.

Access returns within a few minutes of a successful payment.

## Changing the billing email

Owners and admins can change the billing email in **Settings > Billing > Contact**. Support can also change it in chat after verifying your identity. Future invoices go to the new address. Past invoices are not sent again.

## Tax

Invoices show sales tax or VAT where it applies. To add a VAT ID, open **Settings > Billing > Tax details**. A VAT ID added after an invoice was issued does not change that invoice.
